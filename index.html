EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

How to read The Vault

The archive distinguishes what a record is, what it asserts, and what has been independently established.

01

Primary document

An original government, legislative or judicial record. Authenticity does not make every statement inside it true.

02

Assertion

A discrete factual claim made by a source. Assertions can be supported, disputed, contradicted or unresolved.

03

Finding

A formal determination by a court, inspector general or other authorized body, within the limits of its jurisdiction.

04

Interpretation

Analysis of meaning or consequence. Interpretation is labeled and kept separate from the underlying record.
